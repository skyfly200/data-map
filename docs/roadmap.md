See `tasks/WANT-17.md`, `access-sources.md`.
- `WANT-18` **AlphaEarth satellite embeddings (maybe)** — Google's `GOOGLE/SATELLITE_EMBEDDING/V1/ANNUAL` (64-band unit vectors, 10 m, annual from 2017, CC-BY 4.0). Shipped: `habitat-similarity` map layer (mean embedding at a taxon's finds, or at up to 10 spots tapped on the map and shared via `?hs=`; cosine similarity per pixel; unverified against live EE, default 70% floor is a guess). Maybe, not started:
  - **MaxEnt predictors** — add the embedding to `MAXENT_PREDICTORS` (`netlify/lib/maxent.mjs`), reduced to 8–16 PCA components to limit overfitting; compare AUC against the named-variable set. Components are uninterpretable, so variable contribution and response curves mean nothing for them.
  - **Foray planner phase 5** (`WANT-17`) — use embedding similarity to known patches as the "promise" signal for under-sampled cells, and distance from the training sites' embeddings as the training-envelope check. Keep phase 3's habitat score on named predictors, which need readable ranges.
  - **Per-observation enrichment** — an `ee_enrich.py` stage sampling `A00`–`A63` at each point for its observation year (clamped to 2017+), stored with pgvector for "finds from similar habitat" queries. Needs a migration; ~9.6k × 64 floats.
- `WANT-19` **Season forecast from WeatherNext (document only)** — predict when each species' remaining season starts, peaks and ends near a place, as dates with a likely range. Source: Google DeepMind WeatherNext in Earth Engine, `projects/gcp-public-data-weathernext/assets/weathernext_2_0_0` (64 members, 0.25°, 6-hourly to 15 days, from 2022) or `weathernext_3_0_0_0p1deg` (0.1°, hourly, ensemble percentiles only; full members in GCS). Experimental; needs the WeatherNext Data Request form on our EE project; real-time data is under GDM's experimental terms, history CC-BY 4.0. Plan:
  - **Season-to-date series** — the prerequisite. `phenology.ts` stitches weather from each find's 7-day lead-up, so it only covers days people recorded. Add an EE job that pulls a continuous ERA5-Land (temp) + CHIRPS (rain) daily series per 0.25° cell from 1 Jan, the same sources `ee_enrich.py` uses.
  - **Per-species triggers** — fit the accumulation each species fruits at (GDD above `GDD_BASE`, trailing 30/60-day rain) plus an end condition (first hard frost, e.g. tmin < -2 °C for 2+ nights, or cooling GDD rate), using past years' series and `timingByYear`. Keep only species where `thresholdTest` beats the calendar date; the rest fall back to the current day-of-year window in `useInSeason.ts`.
  - **Forecast** — append each WeatherNext 2 member's next 15 days to the season-to-date series (accumulate per member; ensemble percentiles do not add across days), bias-correct against ERA5-Land/CHIRPS by quantile mapping on the 2022+ hindcasts, then extend past day 15 with past years' ERA5 for the same dates (analog years). Run the trigger on every member × analog to get a distribution of start/end dates.
  - **Output** — per species and cell: "starts ~Oct 22 (Oct 18–29)", "likely over by ~Nov 10", probability fruiting in the next 7/14 days. Feeds the Now Fruiting card, `/foray` date planning (`forayScore.ts` plan day/window), and a timeline in the phenology panel. Refresh daily after the 00z run (~07:30 UTC) as a scheduled job; cache per cell.
  - **Validate** — hindcast 2022–2025: issue forecasts from past WeatherNext runs and score predicted vs actual iNat/GBIF onset (median abs error in days) against the climatology-only baseline. Ship only where it wins.
  - Caveats: skill beyond ~10 days is low, so most of a multi-week outlook is climatology; a 0.25° cell is ~28 km, so elevation matters (lapse-rate adjust temp with SRTM); iNat timing is biased by when people go out (`relativeTiming`).
